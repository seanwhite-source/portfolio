About Me

I'm a Mechanical Engineer with a BS from Merrimack College. I currently work as a Laser Applications Engineer, bridging the gap between digital design and physical manufacturing. My professional background is rooted in CAD design, FEA, and GD&T, with plenty of hands-on hours spent in the machine shop. I've always been someone who loves to build, test, and fix things.
